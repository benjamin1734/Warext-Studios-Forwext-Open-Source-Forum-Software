- Existing add-on outbound webhook definition integration.
- `webhook.manage` authorization.
- cPanel-safe database queue worker and protected packaged `bin/webhook-worker.php`.
- Additive webhook platform migration.

## Security invariants

- Raw webhook secrets are held in the encrypted secret store, not webhook DB rows.
- Every delivery attempt re-resolves and re-validates destination addresses.
- Redirects are not followed by the pinned delivery transport.
- Private/reserved destination IPs are rejected.
- Inbound add-on definitions cannot be misused as outbound publishers.
- Delivery logs do not persist response bodies or signing secrets.
- Management operations use the existing Forwext permission engine.
